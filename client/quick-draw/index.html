How Quick Draw works

  • Press Start (or Space). The board goes dark and the border pulses red. Wait.
  • After a random wait of 1.5 to 4.5 seconds, a 2×2 rainbow box appears with one tile empty. Like a box in Easy Prismku, it holds red, blue, yellow and green once each, so the empty tile can only be the one color you don't see.
  • Tap that color in the palette below (or press 1 to 4). Your time runs from the moment the box appears to your tap and is shown to the millisecond (0.001 s). Browsers round their clocks on purpose, so finer digits wouldn't be real.
  • False start: tap before the box appears and the round doesn't count.
  • Wrong color: the first slip adds 1.000 s. A second one ends the round.
  • Your personal best is saved on this device. With a nickname, each finished round goes to the leaderboard. The server deals every round, sets the wait, notes when it showed you the board and when you answered, and turns away times under 0.100 s or ones its own clock can't back up.
